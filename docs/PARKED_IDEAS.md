## From a look at other payroll products (Sage and similar self-service homes)

Not in the agreed Standard tier, so not built. Raise on the review call.

- **Employees view and update their own personal details** (address, bank details, emergency contact), with the payroll office approving changes. Common in other products. The proposal lists only payslips, leave balances and deductions for the portal.
- **Messages or notifications tile** (for example "your payslip is ready", reminders from the payroll office).
- **Leave requests and approvals.** Already out of scope (Premium): only balances are shown.
- **Customisable home page**: employees show, hide or reorder their own tiles.
- **Manager role** with a view of direct reports (headcount, job, length of service). The proposal has three roles only.
